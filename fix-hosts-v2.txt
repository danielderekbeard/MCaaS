FINAL FIX - Use 127.0.0.1
========================

THE REAL PROBLEM:
192.168.127.2 is inside WSL and not accessible from Windows.

THE REAL SOLUTION:
Use 127.0.0.1 - Rancher Desktop's host-switch.exe proxies traffic
from localhost to the cluster.

STEPS:
=====

1. Open Notepad as Administrator

2. Open C:\Windows\System32\drivers\etc\hosts

3. REPLACE the 192.168.127.2 entries with:
   
   127.0.0.1 shuffle.mcaas.example.com
   127.0.0.1 zammad.mcaas.example.com
   127.0.0.1 ciso.mcaas.example.com
   127.0.0.1 wazuh.mcaas.example.com

4. Save and close

5. In PowerShell (as regular user), run:
   ipconfig /flushdns

6. Test in browser:
   https://shuffle.mcaas.example.com

It should work now!
